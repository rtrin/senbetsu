## **Detailed Description**

Is your browser drowning in open tabs? Stop wasting time trying to organize them manually. **Senbetsu** is a smart, AI-powered tab manager that automatically sorts your chaos into neatly labeled tab groups with a single click.

Powered by OpenAI, Senbetsu understands the context of your open tabs and categorizes them intelligently, helping you stay focused, reclaim your browser's speed, and reduce memory bloat.

### ✨ Key Features

🤖 **Instant AI Tab Grouping**
Click one button and let AI instantly classify and assign your unsorted tabs into logical, color-coded Chrome Tab Groups based on the actual content and context of the pages you are viewing. 

🗂️ **Seamless Drag & Drop**
Want to move a tab manually? Easily drag and drop tabs between your groups—or into an "Ungrouped" section—directly from the clean, minimalist popup interface. Your Chrome workspace updates in real-time.

⚡ **Memory Usage Tracking**
Identify the tabs slowing down your computer. Senbetsu provides a dedicated overview of how much RAM each tab is consuming, so you can quickly close heavy pages and free up system resources.

🧹 **One-Click Clean Up**
Got a bunch of random links open? Use the "Clean Up" action to instantly categorize and group any floating, unsorted tabs without affecting the curated tab groups you already have open.

🔍 **Quick Tab Navigation**
Can’t find that one specific document? The popup gives you a clean list view of everything you have open. Click any tab name to instantly jump to it, or easily close individual tabs and entire groups without leaving the extension.

### 🔒 Privacy & Security First
Senbetsu relies on your own OpenAI API key to process tab classifications, meaning you have total control over your usage. We do not track, store, or sell your browsing history. Your data is sent directly to OpenAI to generate group names, and no tab information ever touches our servers. 

*(Note: Requires an active OpenAI API key to enable AI tab classification).*
